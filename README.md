A tiny Python program that writes one sentence to `/output/entry.txt`: a single line of at most 240 characters meant to convince a friend who has never coded to let an AI agent build their first app idea on make.fun. The sentence was picked by drafting ten candidates and duelling them pairwise on the bounty judge's own criteria — makes the case in one read, a concrete low-risk reason to say yes now, plain words with no jargon, no overpromising what an agent can do, and the warmth of a person talking to a friend rather than a slogan. A revision round of three fresh challengers then beat the first winner on clarity, and the program was updated to the new line. The drafts and duels are kept in `candidates.md`.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
